03 / Plan with clarity

The right scope
for your project.

Coordinated consultancy

Select an individual service or discuss a complete package covering architecture, structure, interiors and construction guidance.

New home or residential building

Discuss the floor layout, number of storeys, parking, circulation, future expansion and interior requirements as one coordinated brief.

Commercial & mixed-use spaces

Plan circulation, functional zones, service requirements and structural arrangements around the intended building use.

Renovation & interior projects

Begin with the existing layout and condition. Establish the proposed changes and any structural assessment needed before alterations.

What is included in a complete package?

The proposal defines drawings, design stages, revision rounds, 3D outputs, estimates and site visits. Construction execution and specialist services are agreed separately where required.

What information is needed to begin?

Plot dimensions, location, road access, existing drawings or survey, intended use, approximate budget and your preferred timeline. Structural design also depends on suitable site and soil information.
